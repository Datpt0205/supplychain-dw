## 1. What exists (the facts the mapping relies on)

**Aggregate.** `domain/po_case.py`

- `POCase` fields: `id, tenant_id, workspace_id, po_reference, supplier_name, state, created_at, version, interrupted_state`.
- There is no PIC, no SKU or item link, no PO type and no category. `po_reference` and `supplier_name` must not be blank.
- `CaseState` happy path: `po_created → waiting_deposit → deposit_confirmed → pre_production → production → qc → in_transit → arrived_port → waiting_payment → payment_completed → warehouse_receiving → completed`.
- Exception states:
    - `waiting_external`, `blocked` and `manual_review` are interrupts. They save `interrupted_state` and `resume()` returns there.
    - `rework` is entered only by `fail_qc` and left only by `resume_from_rework → production`.
    - `cancelled` is terminal. `TERMINAL_STATES` = {completed, cancelled}.
- `CaseAction` (closed enum, one guarded method each): request_deposit, confirm_deposit, start_pre_production, start_production, send_to_qc, pass_qc, arrive_at_port, request_final_payment, confirm_payment, start_warehouse_receiving, complete, resume_from_rework, resume, fail_qc*, wait_for_external*, flag_blocked*, flag_manual_review*, cancel*. Actions marked * require a reason (`REASON_REQUIRED_ACTIONS`).
- `apply_action()` is the single dispatch point. Each transition is written to `supply_chain.po_case_state_transitions` (from, to, reason, occurred_at).

**Creating a case.** `CreatePOCase(po_reference, supplier_name)`, scope `supply_chain.po_case.write`. Route: `POST /api/v1/supply-chain/po-cases` with `Idempotency-Key`.

**Taking a step.** `AdvancePOCase`:

1. Resolves the tenant's action duties.
2. Requires `supply_chain.duty.<duty>`.
3. If the tenant's approval matrix lists the action, starts the LangGraph HITL graph `workflows/advance_case_graph.py` (worker `supply_chain_advance_case`, A2). The approval type is `supply_chain.case_action.<action>`, and the composition root registers it in `strict_approval_prefixes`, so the requester cannot approve their own step and a comment is mandatory.
4. Otherwise applies the action directly.

**Duties.** `configs/policies/supply_chain_action_duties@1.0.0.yaml`, overridable per tenant.

| Duty       | Actions                                                                                                                |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ordering   | request_deposit, start_pre_production, start_production, send_to_qc, resume_from_rework, request_final_payment, cancel |
| finance    | confirm_deposit, confirm_payment                                                                                       |
| qc         | pass_qc, fail_qc                                                                                                       |
| logistics  | arrive_at_port                                                                                                         |
| warehouse  | start_warehouse_receiving, complete                                                                                    |
| exceptions | wait / block / review / resume                                                                                         |

**Approval matrix.** `supply_chain_approval_matrix@1.0.0.yaml` ships with `approval_required_actions: []`, so nothing needs approval until a tenant turns it on.

**Roles.** Migrations `f35345378e3c` and `2a0ac1ac32e1`:

- Roles: `sc_viewer`, `sc_operator` (ordering + exceptions; opens cases; the only role that triggers a model call), `sc_finance`, `sc_qc`, `sc_logistics`, `sc_warehouse`, `sc_process_admin` (policies only).
- Separation-of-duty rules in `platform.sod_rules`, enforced by a trigger on memberships: rules vs operations, ordering vs payment, receiving vs payment, ordering vs receiving, ordering vs QC. All five can be waived per tenant, with an audit row.
- No Supply Chain role grants `approvals.*`. Approval authority stays on the platform ladder (approver / manager / director).
- There is no R&D, BGĐ, TP Cung ứng or MKT role.

**SLA.** `supply_chain_sla@1.2.0.yaml` plus `sla_policy.py`.

- Milestones and their test durations: bm04 2d, supplier_confirmation 2d, deposit 1d, port_arrival 2d, payment 1d, warehouse_receipt 1d. All are marked `confirmed`.
- Elmich's reference numbers (4/5/10/21/10/5 days) exist only in a comment, and Elmich never confirmed them.
- `supplier_update`: reminder after 1d, escalation after 2d.
- `domain/sla_evaluation.py` maps four milestones to states, and the clock runs from **entering that state**:
    - WAITING_DEPOSIT → deposit
    - IN_TRANSIT → port_arrival
    - WAITING_PAYMENT → payment
    - PAYMENT_COMPLETED → warehouse_receipt
- bm04 and supplier_confirmation are defined but mapped to no state, so nothing reads them.
- Every other state reports `not_applicable`, and so does any interrupted case.
- The policy is one document per tenant. There is **no category dimension**.

**Follow-ups and notifications.**

- `domain/follow_up.py`: kinds update_reminder, update_escalation, sla_breach. They are opened and resolved by the worker sweep (`apps/worker/.../consumers/supply_chain.py`).
- Recipients are chosen **by scope** (`supply_chain_follow_ups@1.0.0.yaml`), never by person, and are stamped onto the follow-up row.
- Notifications are in-app only. Zalo and email are not wired.

**Daily brief.** `domain/daily_brief.py` and `supply_chain_brief@1.0.0.yaml`. There are 11 signal groups, the tenant sets their order, and an optional AI summary has every sentence checked against the groups it cites.

**AI (model interprets, code decides).** Prompts under `configs/prompts/supply_chain/`:

- `supplier_update_understanding@1.0.0`: event_type (7 provisional values), a verbatim `source_ref`, and `confidence`. Below 0.7, or when the quote is not found verbatim, the update needs a person to confirm it.
- `delay_impact_analysis@1.0.0`: code works out which milestones are affected; the model only lists assumptions and options.
- `case_query_understanding@1.0.0`: the command bar.
- `daily_brief_summary@1.0.0`.

**Tables** (`supply_chain` schema, RLS): `po_cases`, `po_case_state_transitions`, `supplier_updates`, `delay_impact_analyses`, `follow_ups`.

- There is **no documents table and no object storage per case**. The plan file says so ("Evidence partial", "Document Completeness not built").
- Suppliers have no master record; they are matched by exact name.

**Web.** `apps/web/app/supply-chain/*` has the PO case list and workspace, attention queue, control tower, daily brief and follow-ups. These pages were built **before the 2026-09-28 Ant Design decision**, on custom components. Under `CLAUDE.md`'s Web UI rules they must be rebuilt in antd.

## 3. Design for stage 1 (steps 1-9) and the "ĐẶT HÀNG" hand-off

I assume stage 1 lives inside `dw_supply_chain`, so no Protocol is needed for the hand-off. It also follows the existing patterns: a dataclass aggregate with guarded methods, a closed action enum, one dispatch table, duties as policy, the tenant overlay, and RLS-scoped tables.

### Entities (new `supply_chain` tables, all with tenant_id and workspace_id, RLS FORCEd)

- **`ProductDevelopmentCase`** (`product_dev_cases`). One proposed product per case; the step-1 "danh sách SP đề xuất" is a batch of these.
    - Fields: `proposal_code`, `product_name`, `image_object_key` (path carries tenant/workspace), `category` (needed for SLA per category), `supplier_name` (or a supplier id once a master exists), `pic_user_id`, `state`, `interrupted_state`, `sample_round` (int), `cancel_reason`, `version`, `created_by`, `created_at`.
    - `pic_user_id` is **stamped at creation from `context.principal_id`**, NOT NULL, and never re-derived.
- **`SampleRound`** (`product_sample_rounds`): round_no, requested_at, received_at, evaluated_by, result (CHECK: `passed | needs_revision | rejected`), evaluation_note, evaluation report document id. A UNIQUE constraint on (case, round_no).
- **`SampleRevisionRequest`** (the phiếu yêu cầu chỉnh sửa): round_no, requested changes, sent_at, sent_by, document id.
- **`ItemCode`** (`item_codes`): `code`, case id, status, created_by.
    - **UNIQUE (tenant_id, code)**. The database owns duplicate detection, and the adapter maps the violation to a `ConflictError` by constraint name, the same pattern the SoD code uses. A UI pre-check is only a convenience.
- **`Sku`** (`skus`): `sku_code`, `item_code_id` FK NOT NULL `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, variant attributes, `quantity` > 0.
    - UNIQUE (tenant_id, sku_code).
    - "SKU only after the official item code" is enforced by the NOT NULL FK, plus a guard that the item code is `official`, and the aggregate refuses `add_sku` before `issue_item_code`.
- **`CaseDocument`** (`case_documents`), shared with PO cases: `case_kind` + case id, `doc_type` (CHECK over the PDF's list: proposal_list, sample, sample_evaluation, sample_revision_request, product_profile_bm04, official_item_code, po, deposit_docs, payment_docs, packaging_content, user_manual, maquette, plus supplier_confirmation_email), object key, uploaded_by, version.
    - This also fills the "Evidence partial" gap on PO cases.
    - Files go behind the existing S3 port (SeaweedFS).

The approval actions reuse `advance_case_graph`'s shape: interrupt, decide, apply through one `apply_product_action` dispatch. A strict prefix `supply_chain.product_action.` is added at the composition root with `|=`.

**Not yet solved:** the platform approval inbox has no "only BGĐ may decide approval type X" rule. Approval authority today is the platform ladder. Restricting who decides, by role or by duty, is a platform gap to confirm before building.

### Hand-off at "ĐẶT HÀNG" (one transaction, outbox event)

- `PlaceOrder` moves the product case to `ORDERED` and creates the stage-2 record.
- **Problem:** step 10 ("Tạo PO") comes after step 9, but `POCase` requires a non-blank `po_reference`, and the PO number does not exist yet at step 9.
- **Recommendation:** add an initial state `ORDER_REQUESTED`, with `po_reference` nullable until a `create_po` action (step 10, ordering duty).
    - `create_po` sets `po_reference` and `order_kind`, then moves to `PO_CREATED`.
    - A CHECK requires `po_reference` to be set whenever the state is not `ORDER_REQUESTED`.
- New `po_cases` columns:
    - `product_dev_case_id` (FK, nullable, `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, indexed). It is null for a "Hàng đặt lại" not born in stage 1.
    - `order_kind` (CHECK `new | reorder`).
    - `pic_user_id`, **copied from the product case** (a stamp, not a lookup).
    - `category`.
- Planned PO lines: SKU and quantity, referencing `skus`.
- An outbox event `supply_chain.product.ordered` lets the MKT system (out of scope) subscribe later without a direct call.
- The idempotency key is the product case id, so a double click cannot create two orders.

### PIC rule

- `pic_user_id` is stamped at step 1 and carried to the PO case. It replaces "PIC theo ngành hàng".
- Follow-up routing today is scope-only. Add a recipient kind `pic` to `supply_chain_follow_ups` so that SLA breaches and reminders reach the PIC plus the stamped scopes. It is still stamped on the follow-up when it opens.
- Reassigning the PIC (leave, resignation) needs an explicit, audited `reassign_pic` action.

### SLA

- Map `bm04 → PROFILE_IN_PROGRESS` and `supplier_confirmation → SUPPLIER_CONFIRMATION`. This finally gives the two dead keys a reader.
- Change the policy schema to `default` plus `by_category: {category: {milestone: ...}}`, with the case's category passed to the evaluator.
- Add milestones for the states that have none today (sample stages, production, qc, the step 12 sub-steps), filled from the category table.
- Keep `pending_business_confirmation` until Elmich confirms the numbers.
- The current default numbers are 1-2 day test values. Elmich's 4/5/10/21/10/5 must go in as that tenant's own override.

### Duties and roles

- New duties: `rnd`, `sourcing` (or reuse `ordering`), `supply_lead` (TP Cung ứng confirmations). BGĐ and Kế toán act through approvals.
- New role `sc_rnd`. A TP Cung ứng role or an approval scope.
- Possible SoD rule: the R&D person who evaluates a sample is not the one who approves it.

### Step 12 (stage 2, later)

Model it as a `PackagingDesign` sub-flow, with its own states and approvals, hanging off `PRE_PRODUCTION`. `start_production` is then guarded on the sub-flow having reached "R&D test trước SX passed". Its MKT part waits for Part B.
